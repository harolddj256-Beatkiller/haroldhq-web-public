Download being prepared

Harold activates your computer

Downloading alone does not unlock the app.

  1. Install and open Radio Studio.
  2. Press Copy ID and send your Computer ID to Harold.
  3. Receive your licence file, then press Open licence file.
How does the trial work?

Harold issues a 30-day licence for your computer. It includes all features available in this beta, including stems. The 30 days start when the licence is issued. A licence for another computer will not activate yours.

Your approved computer can open the app offline. When the licence expires, you need a new licence to open a new session. Expiry will not interrupt a broadcast already running.

Before you install

This is a beta for testing. Use a test computer and check your audio setup before going on air. The installers are not yet publisher-signed or Apple-notarized, so your computer may ask for approval before opening them.

Stems are included on both versions. Processing speed depends on your computer.
